Questions people ask

What is Skinpal?

Skinpal is an app and community for people with eczema and topical steroid withdrawal (TSW). You log itch, sleep, mood and where your skin flares, by tapping or by talking, and Skinpal shows your own patterns over time. It is made by TSW Nordic AB in Gothenburg, Sweden, and was founded by Lukas Löfgren, who has lived with TSW since January 2022.

How do I keep track of TSW or eczema symptoms day to day?

Log once a day. In Skinpal that takes one tap if today is like yesterday, or a few spoken sentences. A calendar coloured by your itch then shows good and bad periods at a glance.

Can I share my eczema history with my doctor?

Yes. Skinpal creates a 90-day report from your logs: worst days, sleep, body areas and what you applied. You choose whether notes and photos are included.

Does Skinpal diagnose or treat eczema or TSW?

No. Skinpal is a tracking tool. It helps you notice patterns in your own logs and share them. Decisions about treatment belong with you and your doctor.

Is my voice recorded when I use voice logging?

The mic only listens when you tap it. Your words become a log entry and the audio is never stored.

Is there a community in Skinpal?

Yes. Skinpal has a community inside the app where people living with eczema and TSW ask questions, share small wins and check in with each other.

Is my data safe?

Your data is encrypted with AES-256, it is never sold, and you can delete your account and all your health data at any time.

What does Skinpal cost?

Skinpal is free for 14 days. After that it costs $3.99 a month or $39.99 a year.

Which phones and languages does Skinpal support?

iPhone and Android, in English and Spanish.
